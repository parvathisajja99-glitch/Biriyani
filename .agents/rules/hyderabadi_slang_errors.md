# Hyderabadi Street Slang Error Transformation Rule

## Rule ID: `hyderabadi-street-slang-errors`
**Version:** 1.0  
**Context:** Hyderabad Biryani Battle & Real-Time Group Dining Assistant  
**Objective:** Intercept and translate sterile, boring system error messages, warnings, and alerts into authentic Hyderabadi street slang (Dakhni dialect) with humorous hyper-local wisdom (traffic, parking, raita disputes, and hunger urgency).

---

## 1. Core Vocabulary & Syntax Guidelines

When generating or translating error messages, apply these authentic Hyderabadi dialect patterns:

| Dialect Term | Meaning | Tone & Context |
|---|---|---|
| **Baigan / Baigan mein milana** | Eggplant (metaphor for ruining things or 'nonsense') | Extreme frustration or empty results |
| **Chicha / Miya / Ustaad** | Uncle / Bro / Boss | Informal addressing of friends or culprits |
| **Nakko** | No / Don't do it | Strict refusal or warning |
| **Hao** | Yes / Okay | Agreement or affirmation |
| **Lafda** | Mess / Dispute / Brawl | Danger or conflict alert |
| **Challan** | Traffic police fine | Parking or speed violation warnings |
| **Dum pe hai** | Under steam pressure | Loading, waiting, or slow operations |
| **Pait mein chuhe daud rahe** | Rats running in stomach | Extreme hunger / urgency |
| **Khabardar** | Beware / Caution | Critical rule violation |
| **Aakhri / Kirrak** | Supreme / Mind-blowing | Maximum praise or quality indicator |
| **Degh** | Traditional large biryani cooking pot | Inventory / availability |

## 2. Transformation Rules & Error Mapping Matrix

Whenever a standard system error occurs, map it directly using the following transformation table:

### A. Parking & Vehicle Errors
- **Standard**: `ERROR 403: Parking space unavailable at destination.`
- **Hyderabadi Slang**:
  > *"Arey Miya! Udhar car leke gaye toh seedha Challan katega ya auto wale se ghamasaan lafda! Bike pe aao ya metro pakdo, Panjagutta mein car parking baigan mein milti!"*

- **Standard**: `WARNING: No dedicated valet service detected.`
- **Hyderabadi Slang**:
  > *"Valet-walet kuch nai hai chicha, footpath pe lagaye toh traffic police ka crane gadi utha leke bhaag jayega!"*

---

### B. Traffic & Gridlock Warnings
- **Standard**: `ALERT: High traffic congestion. Estimated delay: 45 minutes.`
- **Hyderabadi Slang**:
  > *"Begumpet signal aur Panjagutta flyover pe aakhri level jaam hai bhai! Pait mein chuhe daud rahe toh idhar nakko, raste mein bhook se behosh hojate!"*

- **Standard**: `ALERT: Route passes through Old City narrow lanes.`
- **Hyderabadi Slang**:
  > *"Nayapul se Charminar tak auto wale andha cut maarte! Sedan leke ghuse toh shaam tak wahi biryani ka khwab dekhte baitna padta!"*

---

### C. Food & Restaurant Specific Rules
- **Standard**: `WARNING: Customer requested additional side dish (Curd/Raita).`
- **Hyderabadi Slang**:
  > *"Khabardar! Meridian mein extra raita maangne ka nai ustaad! Purana kissa yaad hai na? Chupchap salan se kaam chalao!"*

- **Standard**: `ERROR 404: Biryani stock depleted / Kitchen closed.`
- **Hyderabadi Slang**:
  > *"Arey degh khali hogayi chicha! Handi saaf kardiye! Baigan ke baatan nakko karo, doosra spot select karo jaldi!"*

- **Standard**: `WARNING: User selected commercial/tourist biryani joint (e.g. Paradise).`
- **Hyderabadi Slang**:
  > *"Arey miya, tourist banre kya? Cardboard biryani khana hai toh jao! Asli Hyderabadi Shadab, Bahar, ya Chicha's jaate!"*

---

### D. System, UI & Voting Errors
- **Standard**: `ERROR 429: Rate limit exceeded. Too many user requests.`
- **Hyderabadi Slang**:
  > *"Ustaad! Ek second mein pachas baar button dabare? Thoda sabar karo chicha, biryani dum pe hai, phat ke haath mein aati!"*

- **Standard**: `ERROR 404: No restaurants match the current filter.`
- **Hyderabadi Slang**:
  > *"Baigan mein miladiye pura search filter! Itna nakhre kare toh ghar pe Maggie bana ke khao! Thoda distance badao jaldi!"*

- **Standard**: `ERROR: Voting stalemate / 50-50 tie detected.`
- **Hyderabadi Slang**:
  > *"Arey dono barabar aagaye! Dawat mein lafda karwate kya? Toss chodo, seedha Handi Spinner ghuma ke kissa khatam karo!"*

- **Standard**: `ERROR 401: Location permission denied.`
- **Hyderabadi Slang**:
  > *"GPS band karke biryani dhoond rahe? Kya re pottey, James Bond banre kya? Location on karo ya Ameerpet drop select karo!"*

---

## 3. Formatting Rules
1. Always maintain a humorous, warm, local friend persona.
2. Pair every slang warning with a realistic, practical workaround (e.g., "Take metro", "Spin the wheel", "Order double masala").
3. Keep the exclamation dynamic: use local conversational interjections (`Hao`, `Arey bhai`, `Ustaad`).
